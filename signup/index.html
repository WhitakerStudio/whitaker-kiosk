Privacy and anonymous analytics

To prevent repeated scan notifications when you revisit or restore the same sculpture page, this site stores a scan-specific timestamp in your browser. It is used for 30 days, remains on your device, and is not transmitted to Whitaker Studio or used to identify you. Expired entries are removed the next time a kiosk page is opened.

We record the gallery and signup page viewed, that QR was used, the general operating-system category, and the time of the visit to help measure use. Our scan analytics do not store a visitor identifier, phone number, or full device information.

Your phone number is received only if you choose to send the prepared text. See the SMS Terms and Privacy Notice above.
