Gallery Scan Alerts

SMS Program Terms and Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Gallery Scan Alerts is a recurring automated SMS program operated by Whitaker Studio. It provides participating gallery personnel with alerts and related information when visitors interact with participating sculpture displays. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to cancel at any time.

SMS Program Terms

Program and sender

The Gallery Scan Alerts program is provided by Whitaker Studio. Messages are sent from Whitaker Studio or its messaging service providers on Whitaker Studio's behalf.

What messages you will receive

After enrolling, you may receive recurring automated informational text messages concerning visitor interactions with sculpture displays associated with the gallery selected during signup. These messages may include gallery identifiers, sculpture information, scan activity, program administration, service notices, support responses, and changes affecting the alert service.

How you consent

By sending the prepared gallery-signup text, you request enrollment and consent to receive recurring automated Gallery Scan Alerts and related program messages from Whitaker Studio at the telephone number used to send the signup message. Consent is specific to this program and Whitaker Studio. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any property, goods, or services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to cancel. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out

You may revoke consent at any time by replying STOP to a program message. Whitaker Studio may send one final message confirming that the opt-out was processed. No additional Gallery Scan Alerts will be sent unless you later enroll again. Whitaker Studio will also honor other legally recognized reasonable requests to revoke consent.

Help

Reply HELP to a program message for assistance. You may also contact Whitaker Studio through whitakerstudio.com or text +1 435-236-2244.

Your telephone number

You represent that you are authorized to enroll the telephone number used for signup. If the number is reassigned, disconnected, or no longer controlled by you, please opt out or notify Whitaker Studio.

Availability and changes

Delivery is subject to wireless-network and carrier availability. Whitaker Studio may modify, suspend, or discontinue the program and may update these terms. Material changes affecting consent will be communicated or presented for renewed consent when required.

SMS Privacy Notice

Information collected

When you enroll in or use Gallery Scan Alerts, we may collect:

  • Your mobile telephone number.
  • The gallery and signup command associated with enrollment.
  • The content of messages you send to or receive from the program.
  • Signup, consent, delivery, HELP, and opt-out dates and times.
  • Message identifiers, delivery status, and related technical records.
  • Information you voluntarily provide while requesting support.

How information is used

Whitaker Studio may use this information to:

  • Enroll you and deliver Gallery Scan Alerts.
  • Associate alerts with the appropriate participating gallery.
  • Respond to HELP requests and provide program support.
  • Process and document opt-outs.
  • Maintain evidence of consent and program compliance.
  • Prevent misuse, protect the service, and comply with law.

How information is disclosed

Whitaker Studio may provide information to messaging carriers, messaging-platform providers, hosting providers, database providers, technical support providers, and other vendors that process it for the purpose of operating and securing the program. Information may also be available to authorized personnel responsible for the participating gallery and may be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or the service.

Mobile opt-in information and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention

Whitaker Studio retains SMS program records indefinitely as part of its operational, consent, compliance, and business records unless it elects to delete them or deletion is required by applicable law. After an opt-out, Whitaker Studio may retain the telephone number and opt-out record on a suppression list so the request can continue to be honored and compliance can be documented.

Security

Whitaker Studio uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect SMS program information. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may choose not to enroll, and you may opt out at any time by replying STOP. You may contact Whitaker Studio to request information about SMS program data associated with your number or to request access, correction, or deletion. Whitaker Studio may retain records where permitted or required for compliance, security, dispute resolution, or enforcement purposes.

Children

Gallery Scan Alerts is intended for participating gallery personnel and is not directed to children under 13. Whitaker Studio does not knowingly enroll children under 13 in this program.

Changes and contact

Whitaker Studio may update this notice as its practices or legal obligations change. The date at the top identifies the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, contact Whitaker Studio through whitakerstudio.com or text +1 435-236-2244.
