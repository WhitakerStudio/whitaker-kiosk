SMS Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 5, 2026

When you send a text through a Whitaker Studio sculpture page, Whitaker Studio receives your phone number, message, and information identifying the sculpture and gallery connected with your inquiry. We retain SMS inquiry records indefinitely and may provide them to the gallery representing the sculpture so the gallery can contact you.

Information we collect

If you choose to send a text message, we may collect and retain:

  • Your telephone number.
  • The content of your message.
  • The sculpture and gallery associated with the message.
  • The date and time of the message.
  • Message-delivery identifiers and related technical records.
  • Photographs, videos, audio, or other attachments you send.

Opening a prepared message in your phone's messaging application does not send this information to us. We receive it only after you choose to send the message.

How we use the information

Whitaker Studio may use SMS inquiry information to:

  • Provide automated information about the selected sculpture.
  • Respond to questions and requests.
  • Identify the sculpture and gallery connected with the inquiry.
  • Allow the representing gallery to follow up about your inquiry.
  • Maintain business, customer-service, and inquiry records.
  • Protect the service from misuse and comply with legal obligations.

Information provided to the representing gallery

We may provide your telephone number, message, sculpture information, gallery information, and related inquiry details to the gallery representing the sculpture. The gallery may use this information to contact you about the sculpture and respond to your inquiry.

Service providers and legal disclosures

We use service providers to transmit messages, host systems, store data, and support our operations. These providers may process SMS information on our behalf. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, or investigate misuse.

Retention

Whitaker Studio retains SMS inquiry information indefinitely as part of its customer-service and business records, unless Whitaker Studio elects to delete it or deletion is required by applicable law.

Your choices

Sending the prepared text is optional. You may browse the sculpture collection or contact a gallery through the other options presented on the sculpture page without sending a text.

You may contact Whitaker Studio to ask questions about SMS information associated with your telephone number or to request access, correction, or deletion. Some information may be retained where permitted or required for legal, security, or recordkeeping purposes.

Sale and advertising

Whitaker Studio does not sell SMS inquiry information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. This notice covers responses and follow-up related to your sculpture inquiry; it does not by itself enroll you in unrelated recurring promotional text messages.

Children

This SMS information service is not directed to children under 13, and Whitaker Studio does not knowingly request SMS inquiry information from children under 13.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our practices or legal obligations change. The date at the top identifies the latest version.
