Privacy and anonymous analytics

We record the sculpture and gallery viewed, whether QR or NFC was used, the general operating-system category, and the time of the visit to help measure interest. Our scan analytics do not store a visitor identifier, phone number, or full device information.

The participating gallery may be notified that this sculpture was viewed, but the notification does not identify you.

To prevent repeated scan notifications when you revisit or restore the same sculpture page, this site stores a scan-specific timestamp in your browser. It is used for 30 days, remains on your device, and is not transmitted to Whitaker Studio or used to identify you. Expired entries are removed the next time a kiosk page is opened.

Before You Continue

Your information is not sent until you tap Send in your messaging app. If sent, Whitaker Studio and the gallery representing this sculpture will receive your phone number, message, and the associated sculpture and gallery so they can provide information and contact you about your inquiry.

SMS inquiry information is retained indefinitely. Read the SMS Privacy Notice .
